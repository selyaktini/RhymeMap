How it works

1 · Read

Captions give the lyrics and the timing of every word in a single fetch, so nothing has to be aligned afterwards.

2 · Sound out

Each word becomes phonemes, then syllables split into onset, nucleus and coda — the parts a rhyme is actually made of.

3 · Compare

Syllables are scored on articulatory distance, not spelling, so bit and feet are near and cat and cat are repetition, not rhyme.

4 · Chain

Repeated spans are found whole, so levitatin' and devastatin' read as one five-syllable chain instead of ten loose syllables.
